# Spec Delta

## Purpose
Define observable requirements for `I. Introduction` (`001-introduction`) following the 2020 A–Z SOP (`G–L`) and objective academic framing.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: G–L Narrative Progression in Introduction
`\section{Introduction}` SHALL progress sequentially through `G` (General context), `H` (However/empirical loss & crash evidence), `I` (In literature: 3 generations), `J` (Judgement & core research question), `K` (4 testable Keypoint contributions), and `L` (List the organization of Sections II–V).

#### Scenario: Explicit roadmap paragraph (L) concludes Introduction
- **WHEN** `I. Introduction` finishes listing the four contributions (`K`)
- **THEN** a closing paragraph (`L`) SHALL explicitly map `\ref{sec:related}`, `\ref{sec:design}`, `\ref{sec:experiment}`, and `\ref{sec:conclusion}` to Sections II–V.

### Requirement: Objective Motivation and Decoupling Framing
`I. Introduction` SHALL state verified motivation bounds without overclaiming guaranteed profit, and SHALL distinguish deterministic geometry, calibrated ML inference, constrained LLM CoT reasoning, programmatic risk gating, PIT isolation, and 3-level failure handling across its contributions.

#### Scenario: Empirical citations respect external validity
- **WHEN** SEBI (87.7% FY26 loss), Reuters (\$19B+ Oct 2025 liquidations), Koviazin et al. (9.0--9.4pp variance), and FinForesight (25/27 leaked intervals; 76% PIT reduction) are cited
- **THEN** the text SHALL attribute each statistic to its exact market/study scope and present contributions as falsifiable targets.
